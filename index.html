The founder’s story

It began with a search for something more.

Our founder has always loved fragrance. It can change the feeling of a room, bring back a memory and make an ordinary moment feel intentional.

Her search for a fragrance that felt right became Aumera Atelier. Each creation begins with care for the ritual as a whole, from the first spark to the last trace in the air.
