The founder's story

It began with a search for something more.

Our founder has always loved fragrance. It can change the feeling of a room, bring back a memory, and make an ordinary moment feel intentional. But the products she found never quite felt right.

So she kept searching. That search became Aumera Atelier, a brand shaped by her belief that fragrance should feel pure, considered, and wholesome. Each creation begins with care for the ritual as a whole, from the first spark to the last trace in the air.
